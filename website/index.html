Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Smart Reminders

Set one-time or recurring reminders. Nudge notifies you at the right moment so nothing slips through.

Countdown Timers

Attach timers to any task. Watch them count down in real-time on your desktop with visual progress bars.

Task Groups

Organize tasks into collapsible groups. Keep your workspace clean and focus on what matters right now.

Desktop Pin

Pin Nudge to your desktop like a sticky note. Always visible, always accessible, right where you need it.

Export & Share

Export your tasks to JSON or share them with your team. Keep your workflow portable and backed up.

Liquid Glass UI

Beautiful frosted glass panels with dark, light, and OLED themes. Customizable opacity and text size.

Themes

Three faces of
Liquid Glass.

Choose the look that fits your workflow. Every surface is crafted with translucent gradients, soft borders, and a subtle glow.

Dark

The default Liquid Glass. Deep translucent panels with cool blue accents.

Light

Frosted glass on a light canvas. Bright, clean, and easy on the eyes.

OLED

Pure black for OLED displays. Maximum contrast, minimum battery drain.

How It Works

Simple as
1-2-3.

01

Type your task

Just type naturally. Nudge understands dates, times, priorities, and durations from plain text.

02

Set a reminder

Attach a timer or due date with a single click. Recurring tasks repeat on your schedule.

03

Stay on track

Nudge floats on your desktop and gently reminds you when it's time. Never miss a deadline.
